About Cafe 16

Tucked into a quiet stretch of Satya Nagar, Cafe 16 is a small, comfortable space built for good coffee and unhurried time.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick cappuccino before work, catching up with old friends over sandwiches and snacks, or settling in with a book for the afternoon, Cafe 16 is designed to feel like a familiar second home in the middle of Bhubaneswar.

Every cup and plate is made with care — nothing fussy, just food and drink that make you want to stay a little longer.
